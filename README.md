# Tata Retail Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

1.Project tittle and / Headline
   Tata Retail Sales Analysis 
   Project Overview :-A brief introduction explaining what the project is about and what business problem it solves.

2. Short Description / purpose:-
 The Tata Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze retail sales performance across different months, customers, and  countries. It provides insights into revenue generation, product quantity, customer contributions, and geographical sales distribution.
 Business Problem Retail businesses need to understand their sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, and geographical revenue distribution to make   better business decisions.

  
3. Tech Stack
- Power BI:Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- Power Query:Data cleaning and transformation (if used).
- Kaggle:Dataset source.
- CSV:Data format.

4. Data Source
- Source: Kaggle.
- Dataset: Retail transaction dataset.
- Format: CSV.
- Includes customer, quantity, revenue, unit price, country, and transaction details.

5. Feature / Highlights
1. Business Problem :-
- Retail businesses need to understand their sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, and geographical revenue distribution to make better business
- This dashboard addresses the challenge of analyzing large volumes of retail data and presenting meaningful insights through interactive visualizations.

2. Goals of Dashboard :-
- Interactive sales performance monitoring.
- Monthly revenue trend analysis.
- Top customer identification.
- Country-wise sales comparison.
- Geographical sales insights.
- Easy-to-understand KPI visualization.

3.Walk through of key visuals:-
  Charts & Visualizations Used -:
  1.KPI Cards: Display key metrics such as Total Quantity, Total Revenue, Unit Price, and Customer Count.
  2.Line Chart:Shows monthly revenue trends from January to December.
  3.Bar Chart:Compares revenue generated by different customers.
  4.HorizontalBarChart:Displays country-wise sales quantity and revenue comparisons.  

 4.Business impact & Insights:-
 1.Business Impact- Helps management monitor overall retail performance.
- Supports identification of high-value customers.
- Helps identify strong-performing geographical markets.
- Makes monthly sales comparisons easier.
- Supports better sales planning and business decision-making.
 2. key Insight :-
  November recorded the highest revenue.
- December experienced a revenue decline.
- Customer ID 14646 was the top revenue-generating customer.
- The Netherlands recorded the highest quantity among the displayed countries.
